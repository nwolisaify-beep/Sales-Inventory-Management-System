# Sales & Inventory Management System

## Overview
The Sales & Inventory Management System is a relational database project designed to manage customers, suppliers, products, orders, payments, and inventory efficiently. The project demonstrates database design, SQL querying, and business intelligence by analyzing sales performance and generating actionable insights.

## Objectives
- Design a normalized relational database.
- Manage sales and inventory records efficiently.
- Retrieve meaningful business insights using SQL queries.
- Analyze revenue, customer behavior, product performance, and supplier efficiency.
- Support business decision-making through data-driven recommendations.

## Database Structure
The system consists of seven related tables:
- Customers
- Suppliers
- Products
- Orders
- Order Details
- Payments
- Sales Inventory Database

## Technologies Used
- MySQL
- SQL
- Relational Database Design
- SQL Joins
- Aggregate Functions
- Subqueries
- Window Functions

## Dataset Summary
- 20 Customers
- 10 Suppliers
- 30 Products
- 40 Orders
- 60 Order Details
- 40 Payments
- Over 170 records inserted

## Key SQL Queries
The project answers several business questions, including:
- List all customers and their orders
- Calculate total revenue
- Identify the top five best-selling products
- Find products that have never been sold
- Rank customers by spending
- Determine the best-performing supplier
- Analyze monthly sales
- Identify above-average spending customers
- Identify premium-priced products

## Business Insights
- Total Revenue: ₦15,617,000
- Average Order Value: ₦390,425
- Electronics generated the highest revenue.
- Speaker was the best-selling product.
- Ava Scott was the highest-spending customer.
- Tech Supplies Ltd was identified as one of the best-performing suppliers.

## Recommendations
- Restock high-demand products such as Speakers, Mouse, and Rice.
- Improve sales of slow-moving products through promotions.
- Reward loyal customers with loyalty programs.
- Negotiate better pricing with top-performing suppliers.
